FAQ

Common questions, answered clearly.

If you have a question that is not covered here, our team can help.

What freight does Might handle?

We support a broad range of freight including truckload, refrigerated, flatbed, LTL, expedited, and intermodal transportation.

Do you work with owner-operators?

Yes. Might supports carrier relationships as well as dedicated dispatch services for eligible owner-operators and fleets.

Can I request a quote online?

Yes. Use the Request a Quote page and provide the shipment details you have available.

Do you offer dispatch services?

Yes. Our dispatch service is designed around load search, broker communication, paperwork, trip support, and operational assistance.

Do you work with recurring lanes?

Yes. We can discuss recurring, dedicated, and ongoing transportation requirements.

How do carriers get started?

Start with the Become a Carrier page and tell us about your company, equipment, and operating profile.
