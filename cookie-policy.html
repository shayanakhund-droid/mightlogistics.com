What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites remember preferences, provide functionality, understand usage, and improve the user experience.

How We May Use Cookies

Might Logistics may use cookies and similar technologies for essential website functionality, security, remembering preferences, measuring website performance, and understanding how visitors use our website.

Types of Cookies

Essential cookies: These may be necessary for core website functionality and security.

Preference cookies: These may remember settings or choices made during your visit.

Analytics cookies: These may help us understand traffic and website usage so we can improve the site.

Third-Party Services

Some website features or services may be provided by third parties and may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Their use is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Managing Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of parts of the website.

Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy when our website, technology, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
